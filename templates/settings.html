The recovery credential is generated automatically, delivered to the developer's security email, and stored only as a cryptographic hash.

⚠️
Important — Developer Recovery is NOT a backdoor

The recovery credential does not directly open the administrator dashboard. It only authorizes an emergency password reset, after which existing sessions are revoked and normal administrator login is required. Never share the credential publicly, and always keep it confidential.

🔒
Security & Accountability

Every recovery operation (generation, delivery, success, failure, lockout, and password reset) is recorded in the Audit Log. The credential is generated with a cryptographically secure random generator, hashed with PBKDF2, and never stored, displayed, logged, or exposed through frontend code.

🔄 Emergency Recovery Workflow

Forgot Password

Administrator cannot sign in and contacts the authorized developer.

Developer Verifies

Developer confirms the administrator's identity/authorization.

Credential Entry

Administrator enters the recovery credential supplied by the developer.

Verification

System verifies the credential hash and authorizes a temporary reset.

New Password

Administrator sets a new strong password; old sessions are revoked.

Audited & Notified

The event is logged, and the developer is notified. Normal login resumes.
